Case study

KAAI

AI agents that support human call center agents during live calls, taken from business need to a full product in production within a few months.

My role

Process lead

Delivery

Full product in production within a few months

Outcome

Contributed to lower operational cost, according to senior management

The product

KAAI supports human call center agents during live calls, in two ways.

Answering the human agent's questions

The human agent types a prompt, and KAAI finds the matching information and delivers it back.

  1. 1. Ask

    The human agent asks a question in a prompt.

  2. 2. Retrieve

    The prompt is used to match and retrieve chunks from a vector database.

  3. 3. Answer

    The retrieved information is delivered back to answer the question.

Next best action (NBA)

An active listening agent follows the conversation and suggests prompts proactively, before the human agent has a chance to type them.

  1. 1. Listen

    The agent listens to the live conversation.

  2. 2. Suggest

    It suggests the next prompt to the human agent.

What I was accountable for

The work spanned many disciplines. My job was to keep them moving toward the same goal and keep our eye on the ball.

  • Stakeholder management, including handling ad hoc requests
  • Scoping business requirements and translating them into technical requirements
  • Handling priorities across the work
  • Escalating relevant topics to senior management
  • Keeping up team motivation and happiness

Two decisions that shaped the project

Improving how we worked

I advocated for retrospectives. My colleagues and our international consulting party came to the office from different countries to hold them, and together we established a better way of working. Optimizing the process was part of delivering the product.

Delaying NBA when it fell short

NBA did not perform as well as we wanted. My colleagues, our international consulting party and I decided unanimously to delay its launch and go back to the drawing board. We're now updating it based on the added value in the user experience, to drive adoption.

The result

We built and deployed a full product to production within just a few months, working across many disciplines.

Senior management has shared that the project successfully contributed to lower operational cost for the business.

KAAI is still an ongoing project.
