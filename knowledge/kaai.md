# KAAI case study
Source: /kaai

## What KAAI is
KAAI is an AI product that supports human call center agents during live calls. It is still an ongoing project.

## How KAAI answers questions
The human agent types a prompt. The prompt is used to match and retrieve chunks from a vector database, and the retrieved information is delivered back to answer the human agent's question.

## Next best action (NBA)
Next best action, or NBA, uses an active listening agent. It listens to the live conversation and suggests prompts proactively, before the human agent has a chance to type them.

## Prashand's role on KAAI
I was the process lead. I was accountable for stakeholder management and handling ad hoc requests, scoping business requirements and translating them into technical requirements, handling priorities, escalating relevant topics to senior management, and keeping up team motivation and happiness.

## Improving ways of working
I advocated for retrospectives. My colleagues and our international consulting party came to the office from different countries to hold them, and together we established a better way of working. Optimizing the process was part of delivering the product.

## The decision to delay NBA
NBA did not perform as well as we wanted. My colleagues, our international consulting party and I decided unanimously to delay its launch and go back to the drawing board. We're now updating it based on the added value in the user experience, to drive adoption.

## KAAI results and outcome
We built and deployed a full product to production within just a few months, working across many disciplines. Senior management has shared that the project successfully contributed to lower operational cost for the business.
